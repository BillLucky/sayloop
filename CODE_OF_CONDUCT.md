# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient with contributors of every background and experience level. Discuss code and behavior rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Use synthetic examples when reporting problems. Never publish another person's recordings, learning materials, credentials, or identifying information without consent.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an existing private contact channel; do not expose the affected person's information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports confidentially, listen to affected contributors, and may edit or remove harmful content or restrict participation. Apply these expectations consistently and proportionately.
